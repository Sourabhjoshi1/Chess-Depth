Questions

How do I receive the files?

Right after payment you get a download link by email, and the files stay in your free Gumroad library so you can download them again any time.

Can I pay in rupees with UPI?

Yes. Prices are shown in rupees for buyers in India, and Gumroad also accepts cards and PayPal from other countries in your own currency.

Can I print the worksheets?

Yes. All PDFs are made to print on A4 paper, for your own use and your own students' practice.

Are these the same as the material in your classes?

They come from the same curriculum. Classes add a coach, live feedback and game reviews; the products are for practising on your own.

A product says "Coming soon". Can I get it?

Tap Notify me and send us the WhatsApp message. We'll tell you as soon as it's ready.
