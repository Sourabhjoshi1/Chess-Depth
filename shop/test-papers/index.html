Free PDF download · No sign-up needed · Print on A4

About this product

Every few weeks, ChessDepth students take a test like these. They show what has been learned and what needs another look.

Print the tests, answer without a board, and mark them with the model answers at the back. For every question you miss, the matching lesson is in our free Foundation and Intermediate Worksheets.

What's inside

  • Beginner Evaluation Test 1 (27 questions, 50 marks)
  • Intermediate Evaluation Test 1 (14 questions, 50 marks)
  • Position Test with 10 diagrams from real games
  • Model answers for every question, position answers checked with Stockfish
  • Marking guide

Who it's for

Students who want to check their level, and coaches and parents who want a ready-made test.
