Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear rules for rescheduling, missed classes, cancellations and refunds.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

We want every family to feel confident enrolling with ChessDepth Academy. This policy explains what happens when plans change. If your situation isn't covered here, message us and we'll find a fair solution.

Free demo class

Your first demo class is free. There is nothing to pay and nothing to cancel.

Rescheduling a class

  • Tell us at least 12 hours before a class and we will reschedule it to another time in the same month (or the next month if the month is ending).
  • Classes cancelled with less than 12 hours' notice, or missed without notice, count as taken, except in a genuine emergency such as illness.
  • If we cancel or a coach is unavailable, we will always reschedule the class, or carry it forward to your next month at no cost.

Monthly classes (one-to-one and group)

  • Monthly fees are paid in advance. You can stop at the end of any month; just tell us before the next month's payment.
  • Full refund if you cancel before your first class of the paid month.
  • After classes have started in a month, the fee for that month is not refunded, but unused classes can be carried forward for up to 30 days.
  • You can pause for exams, travel or illness; unused classes stay available for up to 60 days.

Fixed-length programmes

  • Full refund if you cancel before the programme's first session.
  • 50% refund if you cancel after the first session but before the end of the first week.
  • After the first week, programme fees are not refunded, but you may move to a later batch once if one is available.

Chess Lab

  • The Chess Lab is free for ChessDepth Academy students and, during launch offers, for everyone.
  • Paid memberships can be cancelled at any time and stay active until the end of the period already paid. Membership fees already paid are not refunded.

Shop: digital products

  • Our products are digital PDF files delivered by download and email. There is no physical shipping.
  • Free products cost nothing and need no refund.
  • Because digital files cannot be returned, paid products are not refundable once downloaded. If a file is faulty, incomplete or doesn't match its description, tell us within 7 days and we will fix it or refund you in full.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank transfer) within 7 working days of approval. Shop refunds are processed through Gumroad. Your bank may take a few extra days to show the money.

How to ask

WhatsApp or call +91 98230 29262, or email info@chessdepth.in, with the student's name and what you'd like to change. We reply within one working day.
