ChessDepth AI

Turn any chess image into a clean board you can study. Take a screenshot, a photo of a book diagram or a picture of a real board, and get back the position: every piece on the right square, ready to analyse.

What you'll get

A clean 2D board

Every piece recognised and placed on a standard ChessDepth board, whatever the original style.

Coordinates

Files a–h and ranks 1–8, with the board turned the right way round for White and Black.

The FEN

The position in standard notation, ready to copy into Lichess, Chess.com or any chess program.

One-click analysis

Open the position on Lichess or in our board editor to analyse it, play it out or save it for practice.

Example FEN for the position above: r1bqk2r/1pppbppp/p1n2n2/4p3/B3P3/5N2/PPPP1PPP/RNBQ1RK1 w kq - 4 6

How it will work

The same steps a strong player does in a second, broken into steps a computer can learn.

  1. 1

    Find the board

    Locate the chessboard and its four corners anywhere in the image.

  2. 2

    Straighten it

    Correct the angle so the board becomes a flat, top-down square.

  3. 3

    Split into 64 squares

    Cut the board into its 8 × 8 squares.

  4. 4

    Recognise the pieces

    Decide for every square whether it's empty or holds one of the 12 pieces.

  5. 5

    Check the chess rules

    Make sure the position is possible: one king each, no pawns on the back ranks, and so on.

  6. 6

    Draw the result

    Show the clean board with coordinates and the FEN, ready to analyse.

Screenshots and diagrams first
