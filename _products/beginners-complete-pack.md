You know how the pieces move. This pack teaches what to do with them. It starts with the simple routine that stops most lost games, then covers how to start a game, the tactics and checkmates that decide most beginner games, and how to win (or hold) simple pawn endgames.

Every exercise has a hint, and every answer explains why the move works. The puzzles come from real games in the free Lichess puzzle database, and every solution was checked with Stockfish.
