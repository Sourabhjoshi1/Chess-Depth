Most opening books teach you one side. This one explains every opening from both sides, so whichever colour you have, you know what you are aiming for and what your opponent wants.

Each opening gets its main moves, the position on a board, three key ideas for White, three for Black, and a trap or tip worth knowing. Every line was played through with a chess program, and every trap was checked with Stockfish.
