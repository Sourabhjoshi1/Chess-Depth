Pieces come and go, but the pawn structure stays. It tells both players where to attack, which pieces are good and bad, and which endgames to aim for.

This book teaches you to read a position the way strong players do: look at the pawns first. Every diagram shows only the pawns, so you learn to recognise each structure on sight, whatever opening it came from.
