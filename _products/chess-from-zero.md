This pack takes you from "I have never played" to playing a whole game of chess. Each chapter explains one idea in plain words with diagrams, then gives you exercises: name the square, count the knight's moves, find the free piece, spot check, checkmate or stalemate, decide if you may castle, and give your first checkmates.

Every answer is at the back, with a short explanation. The constructed exercises were checked move by move with a chess engine, and the real-game puzzles come from the free Lichess puzzle database.
