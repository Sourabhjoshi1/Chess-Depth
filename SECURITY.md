# Security Policy

ChessDepth Academy takes the safety of our students, parents and coaches seriously. Thank you for helping keep www.chessdepth.in safe.

## What this covers

- The website at **www.chessdepth.in** (this repository)
- Student and coach sign-in (Google sign-in) and the Student Area
- The Chess Lab, quizzes, reviews and the admin pages
- **ChessDepth AI** (it runs in the browser and images are not uploaded, but please tell us if you find a way they could leak)
- Our Firebase / Firestore security rules

Services we only link to (Lichess, Chess.com, Google Forms, WhatsApp, Instagram and so on) are not run by us. Please report problems with them to those services directly.

## How to report a problem

Please **do not open a public GitHub issue** for a security problem, because that shows it to everyone before we can fix it.

Instead, email **info@chessdepth.in** with the subject line **"Security report"** and include:

- what the problem is and which page or feature it affects
- the steps to reproduce it
- what someone could do with it (for example, read another student's data)
- screenshots or a short video, if they help

## What happens next

- We will reply within **3 working days** to confirm we received your report.
- We will keep you updated while we investigate, at least once a week.
- If the problem is real, we aim to fix serious issues as quickly as possible, usually within **14 days**, and will tell you when it is fixed.
- If we decide it is not a security problem, we will explain why.
- With your permission, we are happy to thank you by name once the fix is live.

## Please be responsible

While testing, please:

- only use your own account, and never access, change or delete other people's data
- avoid anything that could slow down or break the site for students
- give us a fair chance to fix the problem before telling anyone else

We are a small academy and cannot offer paid bug bounties, but we are genuinely grateful for every report.
