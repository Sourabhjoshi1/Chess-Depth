Why train blindfold?

Every time you calculate, you move pieces in your head. Blindfold drills make that picture sharper, so you see more moves ahead and blunder less.

10 minutes a day

Do two or three drills a day, before your puzzles. Start on Easy, and move up when you score well three times in a row.

For every level

Kids start with square colours and knight routes. Club players use Follow the Moves and Blindfold Puzzles to stretch their calculation.

Follow the Moves

A real opening is played from the starting position, one move at a time, without showing the pieces. Keep the position in your head, then answer questions about where things stand. On Medium and Hard the board is hidden, and on Hard you only see the latest move.

Tips from our coaches

  • Learn the diagonals. a1–h8 and h1–a8 are the long diagonals. Every square on them is the same colour. Use them as signposts.
  • Say the moves. Reading moves aloud, or turning on Read aloud, helps the picture stick.
  • Check the kings first. When you memorise a position, find both kings, then the queens and rooks, then everything else.
  • Don't rush to Hard. Getting every answer right on Easy builds the habit. Speed comes later.
