ChessDepth ("we", "us") runs this website, chessdepth.in, and offers online chess coaching (as ChessDepth Academy) and online chess tools from Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India. This policy explains what information we collect through the website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, write to {{ site.contact.email }}.

Information you give us

  • Inquiry and contact forms. When you send a form, we receive the details you enter: for example the student's name and age, a parent's name, chess level and rating, country and time zone, WhatsApp number, email address, the course and class times you are interested in, and your message. Forms are delivered to us by Formspree.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email. If you contact us through a WhatsApp link, by phone or by email, we receive your number or address and the messages you send. WhatsApp is operated by Meta under its own privacy policy.
  • Registration form. When you register for classes, you fill in a Google Form. We receive the details you enter, such as the student's name and age, a parent's name, phone and email, country and time zone, chess level, the class format you choose and preferred class times. Google Forms stores the answers in our Google account.
  • Student and payment records. When you join ChessDepth Academy, we keep a student record: the student's name, age, parent's name and contact details, scholar number, coach, programme, level, start date, fee, and the dates and amounts of payments. We use it to run your classes, issue your admission letter and keep our accounts. It is kept privately by us and shared only with the student's coach, as far as the coach needs it to teach.
  • Payments for classes. Families outside India pay through Wise, and families in India by UPI or bank transfer. Wise and your bank handle your payment details under their own privacy policies. We see only the payer's name, the amount and the date, never your card details.
  • Student Area. When you sign in to the Student Area with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google, and we keep a record of when you first and last signed in. This is stored with Google Firebase.
  • ChessDepth AI. Images you open in ChessDepth AI are read inside your browser and are not uploaded. If you press "Send the corrected position", we store 64 small grey squares cut from the board in your image and the corrected position in Google Firebase, with no name, email or account details, and use them to train the recognition model. Only we can read them.
  • Chess Lab. When you sign in to the Chess Lab, we store your training progress (for example puzzle rating, points, level, day streak, puzzles solved and opening-course progress) with your account in Google Firebase. If you link a ChessDepth Academy enrollment number, we store it with your account so the Lab stays unlocked for you. Your first name with your weekly points and your best Puzzle Streak, Puzzle Rush and Coordinates scores appears on the Lab leaderboards, which other signed-in Lab users can see; we never show surnames or email addresses there.
  • Reviews. When you post a review of our academy, a coach, a course, the Chess Lab or a Shop product, you sign in with Google and we store your star rating, your review, the name you choose to show (we suggest your first name and the initial of your surname), whether you are a student, parent or other player, and the dates. Your review, chosen name and role are public on our website as soon as you post them. If you have linked a ChessDepth Academy enrollment number, your review shows a "Verified student" badge. We never show your email address. You can edit or delete your review at any time. We may reply publicly, and we hide reviews only if they contain abuse, spam, advertising or personal details.
  • Linked Lichess account. If you link your Lichess username in the Student Area, we store the username with your account so your report loads each time, and your coaches can see it. Your report is built in your browser from your public Lichess games and ratings, using Lichess's free public API; we never ask for your Lichess password. You can unlink at any time.
  • Shop purchases. Products in our Shop are sold and delivered by Gumroad. When you buy, Gumroad collects your payment details and email address under its own privacy policy, and shares your name, email address and what you bought with us so we can support you. We never see your card or UPI details.

We use this information only to answer your questions, arrange demo classes and lessons, run the Student Area, and keep in touch with you about your classes.

The ChessDepth Assistant

The chat assistant at the bottom of each page runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and questions are not stored or sent to us. If you tap "Send this on WhatsApp" or "Ask on WhatsApp", WhatsApp opens with a message containing your answers, and we receive it only if you choose to send it. We count how the assistant is used (for example, that it was opened or that a class was suggested) in Google Analytics, without the text you type.

Information collected automatically

  • Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are visited, how people arrive (such as from a Google search), what type of device and browser they use, their approximate location (city or country), and which buttons are clicked. Google Analytics uses cookies. It does not tell us who you are. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Embedded content and fonts. The map on our Contact page is provided by Google Maps, and fonts and some page components are loaded from Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs. These services receive your device's IP address when they deliver content to your browser.

Information stored only on your device

Some features remember your choices in your own browser's storage, not on our servers: your night or day mode setting, your blindfold training progress, your Puzzle of the Day streak, your quiz level, and the endgames you tick on the Resources page checklist. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Lichess game analyser

When you use the game analyser on the Resources page, your browser sends the Lichess username you type directly to Lichess to fetch public ratings and games. We do not store the usernames you look up.

Children

Many of our students are children. We ask that a parent or guardian fills in our forms, contacts us, or agrees to their child's use of the Student Area on the child's behalf. If you believe a child has given us personal information without a parent's agreement, please contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above (Google, Formspree, Meta for WhatsApp, Wise, Gumroad and Lichess), which help us run the website and our classes, with your coach as described above, and when the law requires us to.

Where your information is stored

We are based in India. Some of our service providers, such as Google and Formspree, store information on servers in other countries, including the United States. Wherever you live, we handle your information as this policy describes.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and contact details for as long as we need them to respond to you and, if you become a student, for as long as you study with us. We keep payment records for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires. Google Analytics data is kept according to the retention period set in our Analytics account.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing {{ site.contact.email }}.
  • You can delete your reviews, unlink your Lichess username and sign out of the Student Area and Chess Lab at any time.
  • These choices apply to everyone. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under your local law, such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the UK and EU GDPR, and we will respect them.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Security

The website is served over a secure (HTTPS) connection, and we only use well-known service providers to handle forms, sign-ins and payments. Our database only lets each signed-in person read their own account, and student records are visible only to us. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or services change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If a change is significant, we will tell enrolled students on WhatsApp.
