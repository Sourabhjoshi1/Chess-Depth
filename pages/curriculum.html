How it works

One path, in small steps

4 levels

Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced and Elite, matched to rating bands, from first moves to 1900 and above.

3 stages per level

Each stage is about eight sessions on a few connected ideas, so nothing is rushed.

A quiz every 8 sessions

Each stage ends with a quiz in the Student Area, so you and the coach see what has stuck.

Move up when ready

The coach moves a student on when quizzes, games and results show they're ready, not on a fixed date.

The same curriculum runs in one-to-one training and in group classes. Every stage also includes puzzles, practice games and analysis of the student's own games.

Inside a class

What happens in a 60 to 70 minute class

  1. 1

    Warm-up puzzles

    A few puzzles on recent topics, to switch on calculation and check the last lesson stuck.

  2. 2

    The new idea

    One idea from the stage, shown in clear positions, often from classic games. Students explain moves before the coach does.

  3. 3

    Guided practice

    The student uses the idea in new positions, with the coach asking "why?" at every step.

  4. 4

    Play and review

    Practice games or positions played out, and a look at the student's own recent games.

  5. 5

    Homework

    A short worksheet or Chess Lab task to do before the next class.
