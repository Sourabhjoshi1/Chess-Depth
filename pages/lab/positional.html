Positional play

What to do when there is no tactic. Positional chess is the skill of improving your position a little with every move: better pieces, healthier pawns, a safer king. This is the place to learn it, in order, and to practise it.

The positional checklist

When you cannot see a tactic, go through these five questions. The answers give you a plan.

  1. What does my opponent want?Find their best idea first. If it is dangerous, stop it.
  2. Which is my worst piece?Find a better square for it. Repeat until every piece is working.
  3. Where are the weaknesses?Isolated, doubled and backward pawns, weak squares and an unsafe king, for both sides.
  4. What is my pawn break?Play on the side where your pawns point and you have more space.
  5. Which pieces should I trade?Keep your good pieces, exchange your bad ones and your opponent's best defender.
