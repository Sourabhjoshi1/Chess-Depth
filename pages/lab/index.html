Questions

Is the Chess Lab included in ChessDepth classes?

Yes. The Chess Lab is free forever for every student enrolled at ChessDepth Academy. Sign in, enter your enrollment number once, and everything unlocks. Don't know your number? WhatsApp us. Players who don't take classes with us can join with a Chess Lab membership.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything works in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone.

Where do the puzzles come from?

From the free Lichess puzzle database (rated by millions of solves), carefully filtered for quality, plus ChessDepth Originals written by our coaches.

Is my child's information safe?

We only store the name and email from Google sign-in and your Lab progress. The leaderboard shows first names only. See our privacy policy.
